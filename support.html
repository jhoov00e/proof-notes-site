Common questions

Where are my recipes stored? On your iPhone or iPad. If you turn on iCloud sync they also sync through your own iCloud account. Settings has a one-file backup you can save anywhere.

How do I replay the tour? Settings, then Help, then “Take the guided tour”.

Privacy: the app does not collect your data. See the privacy policy.
