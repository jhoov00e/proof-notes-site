Proof Notes: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Proof Notes ("the app") is made by Josh Hoover. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and nothing is sent to the developer.

What stays on your device

Your recipes, notes, ratings, photos, meal plans, shopping lists and timers are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage.

What the app can access, and why

The app asks permission before using any of these, and only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera: to scan recipe cards and cookbook pages. Text is read on your device.
  • Photos: only the pictures you choose to attach to a recipe or an attempt.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: to dictate a recipe or step and for optional voice commands in cook mode. When your device supports it, speech is turned into text on the device. On devices that don't, Apple may process the audio to recognize speech, under Apple's own privacy terms. The app does not keep any recordings.
  • Notifications: to tell you when a kitchen timer finishes and for optional dinner reminders.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: so you can ask Siri to start a timer, add a note to a recipe or read tonight's plan. Siri handles your voice as described in Apple's privacy terms.
  • Live Activities and Widgets: show timers, your shopping list and plan on your Lock Screen and Home Screen. The data they show is shared only between the app and its widgets on your device.

Things you can choose to turn on

  • iCloud sync (off by default): if you turn it on in Settings, your recipes, photos and meal plan are stored in your own iCloud Drive so they sync across your devices. This uses your Apple ID and is governed by Apple's terms. The developer cannot see it.
  • Backups and sharing: when you export a backup, a recipe file or a PDF, you decide where it goes.

Network use

The app connects to the internet only when you ask it to import a recipe from a web address you enter, and then it contacts that website to read the recipe, like a browser would. It does not send your recipes or notes anywhere.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, collects no personal information and has no accounts.
